# 💡 点子库 · Idea Vault · ideas_wsy

> *"Good ideas are not scarce; time and energy are."*

我大概率不会亲自把它们做出来。但这不代表它们应该被忘掉。
*I most likely won't build them myself. But that doesn't mean they should be forgotten.*

## 这里有什么、没有什么 · What's Inside & What's Not

**有 / What's Inside:**
- 被认真写下来的问题、场景和初步思路 (Carefully documented problems, scenarios, and initial thoughts)
- 一些我知道“值得做但轮不到我做”的东西 (Ideas I know are "worth doing, but not my turn to do")
- 一个按状态追踪点子的公开看板 (A public board tracking ideas by status)

**没有 / What's Not:**
- 完整的技术方案或商业计划书 (Complete technical proposals or business plans)
- 承诺“一定会做”的路线图 (Roadmaps promising "this will definitely be built")
- 公司内部信息、客户数据、任何密钥或敏感截图 (Internal company info, customer data, API keys, or sensitive screenshots)

## 点子模板 · Idea Template

一个好的点子 Issue 应该回答清楚四件事：**问题是什么、大致怎么解、对谁有价值、还有什么没想清楚**。
*A good idea Issue should clearly answer four things: **the problem, the rough solution, the value, and the open questions**.*

```markdown
## 🧩 解决什么问题 / The Problem
一句话说清楚：什么东西现在很烂 / 不存在 / 很贵？
*What is currently broken, non-existent, or too expensive?*

## 💡 初步想法（可留空） / Initial Thoughts (Optional)
大概可以怎么做。允许只有一个模糊的念头。
*How it could be done. A vague thought is totally fine.*

## 🎯 对谁有价值 / Target Audience
谁会真的用？为什么？
*Who will actually use this? Why?*

## ❓ 还没想清楚的事 / Open Questions
最大的不确定性在哪里？
*What are the biggest uncertainties?*
```

不必填满。但“解决什么问题”这一栏，请认真写。
*No need to fill everything, but please take "The Problem" seriously.*

## 标签体系 · Labels

| 标签 · Label | 含义 · Meaning |
|---|---|
| `idea` | 这是一个点子 (This is an idea) |
| `seed` | 刚冒出来，还没想清楚 (Just popped up, not fully formed) |
| `exploring` | 在琢磨可行性 (Exploring feasibility) |
| `building` | 已经有人在做了 (Someone is already building it) |
| `parked` | 暂时搁置，但没放弃 (Parked temporarily, not abandoned) |
| `dropped` | 判断不值得做，**必须写一句为什么** (Dropped, **must state why**) |
| `area:ai` / `area:tool` / `area:life` / `area:business` | 领域分类 (Domain categories) |

`dropped` 的 Issue 不会被删除。**一个被认真放弃的点子，比一个被悄悄删掉的点子有价值得多**——它记录了“为什么此路不通”。
*`dropped` Issues are never deleted. **A carefully abandoned idea is far more valuable than a silently deleted one**—it documents "why this path failed."*

## 原则与约定 · Principles & Rules

- **点子廉价，执行值钱。** 这里的目的不是“攒着一堆想法然后觉得自己很厉害”，而是**别让好想法因为没人动手就烂在脑子里**。
  *Ideas are cheap, execution is priceless. The goal here isn't to hoard thoughts and feel smart, but to **prevent good ideas from rotting in our heads due to lack of action**.*
- **不承诺。** 仓库里的点子不构成任何“我会做”的承诺。`parked` 和 `dropped` 是常态，不是失败。
  *No promises. Ideas here do not constitute a commitment to build. `parked` and `dropped` are the norm, not failures.*
- **公开、安全。** 不放公司 / 客户 / 私密信息，不放密钥、内部截图。提交前自己检查一遍。
  *Public & Safe. No company/customer/private info, no keys or internal screenshots. Double-check before submitting.*
- **许可 · License。** 代码模板用 MIT；`ideas/` 下的文档与构想用 CC BY-SA 4.0（[LICENSE-DOCS.md](LICENSE-DOCS.md)）。CC BY-SA 法律上要求署名——但说实话，你要真做出来了不署名，我也不会去追。
  *Code templates: MIT. Documents under `ideas/`: CC BY-SA 4.0. Legally that asks for attribution—but honestly, if you build it and forget to credit me, I'm not coming after you.*
- **欢迎拿走，欢迎实现，欢迎告诉我。** 署名归提交者，实现归动手的人，开心归所有人。
  *Take it, build it, tell me about it. Credit goes to the submitter, realization goes to the builder, joy goes to everyone.*
- **点子不保证能够技术实现。** 这里是“想象力的许愿池”，不是“硬核工程招标会”。拥抱超前的脑洞，但拒绝违背科学。这个仓库不承担“技术可行性验证”的责任。允许技术极度超前、允许当前工程无法实现，但拒绝伪科学和反智主义。真正的异想天开，是比时代早走了十年的远见。
  ***No guarantee of technical feasibility.** This is a "wishing well for imagination," not a "hardcore engineering bidding fair." Embrace cutting-edge wild ideas, but reject pseudoscience. This repo does not validate technical feasibility. Tech can be decades ahead of current engineering capabilities, but pseudoscience and anti-intellectualism are strictly forbidden. True wild imagination is a vision that walks ten years ahead of its time.*
